Scholarship Program

{{ organization.name }} awards a $2,000 scholarship each year to a local student pilot or mechanic advancing their aviation training.

About the Scholarship

The scholarship is intended for advancement in aviation - earning a private pilot license, an advanced rating, or training in aircraft maintenance. Other related aviation training will also be considered.

Eligibility

  • Must live in Monterey County and be at least 16 years old.
  • Applying for flying purposes: must hold a student pilot license and an airman's medical certificate, and must have soloed an aircraft.
  • Applying for an advanced rating: a current flight review is required.
  • Must read, speak, and understand English, per FAA requirements.

How to Apply

Applications are due June 1 of the current year, with the winner announced by July 15. A complete application includes:

  • The application form (below)
  • A personal essay of at least 500 words covering your aviation goals, first interest in aviation, relevant experience, obstacles you've faced, and how you'd like to contribute back to {{ organization.name }} and the aviation community
  • Two letters of recommendation, at least one from someone familiar with your aviation interests and accomplishments

Submit your materials to the Scholarship Committee at scholarship@salinaspilots.org, or by mail to 80 Mortensen Avenue, Salinas, CA 93905.

If You're Selected

Scholarship funds are paid directly to the flight school, training center, or instructor, against submitted receipts and signed hour logs. Recipients are asked to:

  • Attend at least two monthly association meetings during the award year
  • Submit monthly progress reports signed off by an instructor
  • Volunteer for one aviation-related event - an airshow, fundraiser, Young Eagles rally, or airport activity

Recipients who complete their training are also encouraged to write an article or give a presentation about their experience to the club.

Questions?

Stop by a monthly meeting - second Tuesday of each month, Gate 1 in the Salinas Municipal Airport Terminal - and a member of the Scholarship Committee can help.
