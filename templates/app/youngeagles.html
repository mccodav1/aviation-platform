Young Eagles

Introducing the next generation to the joy of flight, alongside our partners at EAA Chapter 204.

What Is Young Eagles?

Young Eagles is EAA's national program giving young people ages 8 to 17 a free introductory flight with a volunteer pilot - often their first time in a small aircraft. It's been welcoming kids into aviation since 1992.

Our Role

{{ organization.name }} supports Young Eagles events with EAA Chapter 204, our local partner. Members volunteer as pilots and ground crew for local Young Eagles rallies.
